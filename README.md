Cora is a Brazilian digital bank (conta PJ) for small and medium businesses, offering fee-free business checking, Pix, boletos, transfers, payment initiation and financial management. Its developer platform exposes REST APIs for registered boleto and carnê issuance, Pix QR code generation, account data, balance and statement queries, payment and transfer initiation (including DARF and GPS tax payments), webhook notifications, and municipal service invoice (NFS-e) issuance. Cora offers two integration modalities — Direct Integration (mutual-TLS certificate plus OAuth2 client-credentials) and Cora Partnership — with a Stage sandbox for testing. Backed by QED Investors and Ribbit Capital.

**Areas:** Company · Banking · Brazil · Payments · Pix · Boleto · Invoicing · Small Business · Fintech · Banking as a Service

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Cora: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
